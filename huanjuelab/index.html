A few things to know

What is free today, and what is planned for later.

Is it free to follow the channels?

Yes. You can subscribe to Huanjue Lab on YouTube or follow our official Instagram and Facebook accounts for free. These social subscriptions are separate from the paid membership we are developing.

Can I join the paid membership now?

Not yet. The membership section is a preview of planned content. Registration, payment and member access are not available. Pricing, benefits, publishing schedules and subscription terms will be announced when confirmed.

What is included in the free starter sample?

The free ZIP contains a complete fictional story, a storyboard with the first three shots filled in and six more rows to complete, a prompt example and blank worksheet, a quick-start guide and a usage licence. All downloadable sample content is in Traditional Chinese. No finished video or audio is included, and no email, payment or subscription is required.

Can I use the free sample for commercial videos?

Yes. The included one-user, non-exclusive licence permits personal or client commercial finished videos. It does not permit sharing or reselling the editable templates. Read the included licence for the full terms, and separately check the rights and terms of any third-party tools or assets you use.

When will the full paid kit launch, and what is included?

There is no confirmed paid launch date. Our focus is on free videos and resources first. A fuller Traditional Chinese kit is planned to include a story pacing guide, reusable storyboard and subtitle templates, a visual prompt framework, a production checklist and asset organization tools. Final contents, licensing, pricing, delivery and refund terms will be provided before any sale. The free sample does not unlock future paid products.

Why are some episodes missing a Facebook link?

Publishing progress varies by platform. We only list links confirmed as public. If an episode has no Facebook link, select YouTube or Instagram to check its available viewing options.

Are the stories based on real events?

三點的實驗室 is an original fictional suspense series made with AI-assisted visuals. Its characters, events and imagery are not presented as records of real people or events.

Does this site take payments or collect personal information?

This site currently has no payment, registration or personal-information forms, and it does not use analytics trackers. Social and video links open third-party platforms, where their own terms and privacy policies apply.
