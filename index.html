About Me

I am a physician-scientist at Taipei Veterans General Hospital with an MD and a PhD in Biophotonics from National Yang-Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), and a background in computer science. My work sits at the intersection of clinical medicine, artificial intelligence and evidence synthesis: I build and evaluate deep-learning models for medical imaging, and I use Bayesian network meta-analysis and diagnostic-test-accuracy methodology to establish what these models actually deliver for patients. Recent work focuses on AI screening for diabetic retinopathy and other retinal diseases, AI in radiology and thoracic oncology, and radiomics-based prognosis. I am currently applying for ophthalmology residency training at Taipei Veterans General Hospital.
